01 · The question

Does “more mature” necessarily mean “more faithful”?

The published study reported lower progenitor / cell-cycle-associated expression and higher maturation-associated expression in organoids cultured in low-Earth orbit. This Build asked a narrower question: can those selected marker directions be recovered from the public NASA processed data?

What this Build tested

Whether TH and DDC shift upward while VIM, SOX2, PAX6, CCND1, CDKN1A and GADD45A shift downward in LEO-associated comparisons.

What this Build did not prove

Functional neuronal maturation, improved Parkinson’s disease modelling, causality from microgravity alone, or a generalizable PD-specific response.

02 · Experimental design

Twenty-three samples, but only two donor-specific iPSC lines.

The design audit changed how the results should be interpreted. Disease label, donor and cell line are fully confounded, so the defensible analysis is primarily within-cell-line Ground-versus-LEO reproduction.

04 · Scientific interpretation
Maturation-associated transcription ≠ functional fidelity.

Higher TH/DDC and lower progenitor-associated expression can be compatible with a shift toward maturation, but bulk RNA-seq alone cannot establish mature electrophysiology, synaptic function, dopamine release, protein abundance, cell-type composition or preserved ventral-midbrain identity.

Observed

Consistent marker-level LEO-associated transcriptional shifts across four matched comparisons.

Still unresolved

Whether the signal reflects within-cell maturation, altered composition, or stress / cell-cycle state.

Next scientific question

Does the phenotype preserve dopaminergic identity, cellular integrity and function required for a faithful PD model?

07 · Scientific integrity

Claims stop where the data stop.

Processed-data provenance

Normalized expression and differential-expression statistics came from NASA-processed outputs; this was not an independent raw-count DESeq2 analysis.

Donor limitation

One control line and one PD line prevent generalizable disease-specific inference.

Spaceflight ≠ microgravity alone

Ground-versus-LEO differences are described as spaceflight-associated rather than attributed automatically to one exposure.
