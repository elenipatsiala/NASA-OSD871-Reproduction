# Expert Outreach

## Short Project Description

I reproduced selected published transcriptional findings from NASA OSD-871, a public RNA-seq dataset of human iPSC-derived dopaminergic organoids exposed to Ground Control or low-Earth-orbit spaceflight conditions.

Across four matched Ground-versus-LEO contrasts, TH and DDC consistently shifted upward in LEO, while VIM, SOX2, PAX6, CCND1, CDKN1A and GADD45A consistently shifted downward. Statistical support varied across markers, with DDC showing the strongest consistency.

The analysis uses NASA-processed normalized counts and NASA-processed differential-expression outputs. I also audited the experimental design and found that the 23 organoid samples originate from only two donor-specific iPSC lines, so disease status is confounded with donor/cell line.

The full analysis was organized into a reproducible R pipeline and successfully rerun from a fresh R session.

## Questions for Expert Feedback

1. Is my distinction between directional consistency and subgroup-level statistical significance scientifically defensible?

2. Given that disease status is confounded with donor/cell line, is it appropriate to frame the analysis primarily as within-cell-line Ground-versus-LEO reproduction?

3. What would be the strongest next analysis or experiment to distinguish true dopaminergic maturation from changes in cell composition or stress/cell-cycle state?
